      contemporary products alongside resources on Shaker design. The project
      draws on the Shakers’ belief that careful work was part of religious
      life, a principle visible in the simplicity and durability of their
      everyday objects. That relationship between belief and practice guides
      the identity.

      quilt star becomes a repeatable arrangement of triangles and a circle,
      while the columns and marginal notes of historic Bible pages provide a
      grid for type and images. Together, the pattern and grid connect the
      visual language of Meeting House to the quilt and the printed page.
